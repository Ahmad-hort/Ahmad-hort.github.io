01 — About

Agricultural Scientist

A dedicated and results-oriented horticulturist holding an M.Sc. (Hons.) in Horticulture (CGPA 3.52/4.00) from the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad — Pakistan's premier institution for agricultural sciences. Academic training encompasses the full scope of horticultural science, from orchard establishment, fruit and vegetable crop production, and integrated pest management to advanced postharvest technology, sustainable agriculture systems, integrated fruit production pipelines, and cold-chain logistics.

02 — Academic Disciplines

Core Areas of Study

The M.Sc. Horticulture curriculum at UAF provided a rigorous grounding across six major horticultural disciplines, building an integrated command of field production, postharvest science, and sustainable crop management systems.

Orchard Management
Establishment, training, pruning, canopy management, nutrient programming, and irrigation scheduling for commercial fruit orchards. Emphasis on citrus, mango, guava, and stone fruits in Punjab's agroclimatic zones.
Fruit Crop Production
Agronomy of major and minor fruit crops including Kinnow mandarin, citrus species, mango, dates, grapes, and tropical fruits. Production systems, variety selection, fertilisation, and harvest management.
Vegetable Production
Open-field and protected cultivation of major vegetable crops. Nursery management, transplanting schedules, nutrient management, water requirements, and production economics under different Punjab agro-ecological conditions.
IPM of Horticultural Crops
Integrated Pest Management strategies combining biological, cultural, physical, and chemical control methods. Economic thresholds, pest monitoring, natural enemy conservation, and minimum-residue pesticide protocols for citrus, vegetables, and orchard crops.
Sustainable Agriculture
Agroecological principles, conservation agriculture, soil health management, water-use efficiency, and climate-smart practices. Reducing chemical inputs while maintaining productivity through organic amendments and precision management.
Postharvest Science & Technology
Harvest maturity indices, postharvest physiology, modified and controlled atmosphere storage, cold-chain management, quarantine treatments, fruit quality evaluation (TSS, TA, firmness, weight loss), and export-quality grading standards.
